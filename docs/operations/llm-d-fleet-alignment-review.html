llm-d-fleet: component ownership and product alignment

The product is best positioned as the fleet eligibility and operations control plane—not a replacement for llm-d Router/EPP, KServe, KEDA, WVA, or cluster-local serving.

The simplest product statement
llm-d-fleet determines which exact-model providers are allowed and healthy across failure domains. The selected data plane and cluster-local llm-d stack decide how to execute within that qualified set.

How to present it

Upstream submissionLead with eligibility, health, exact-model discovery, draining and Router conformance.
Red Hat productKServe serves, EPP scores, KEDA scales, WVA optionally optimizes, fleet coordinates failure domains.
Downstream productionRetain Routes, Praxis, HA, external state, governed evidence and certification overlays.
Do not claimDo not replace EPP/KServe/KEDA/WVA; do not call Brutus-only GPU HA; do not require optional ecosystem tools for OSS.
Messaging guardrails
  • Do not claim fleet replaces EPP, KServe, KEDA, WVA, ModelPlane, or llm-d Router.
  • Do not advertise the Brutus-only 8B model as HA.
  • Do not make DeepField, GCL, ledger, llm-d-sc, Praxis, or OpenShift Routes OSS prerequisites.
  • Do not present mock ModelPlane evidence as an official integration.
